Assignment Criteria & Rules

Working Hours: 9:00 AM – 5:00 PM

Deliveries are strictly scheduled within 09:00 – 17:00. Orders submitted prior to 9:00 AM are queued with high priority.

Lunch Break Protection: 12:00 PM – 1:00 PM

The 12:00 to 13:00 window is reserved exclusively for staff meal breaks and cannot be assigned tasks.

Dynamic Buffer Intervals

Standard 15-minute buffer between tasks. For closely located tasks (< 500m), an optimized 10-minute buffer applies.

Daily Task Cap: 8 Deliveries Per Staff

To ensure safety and service quality, staff members cannot exceed 8 assigned tasks on any calendar day.

Routing & State Boundaries

Live routing calculated through OpenStreetMap OSRM. Delivery staff only operate within their registered home state.
